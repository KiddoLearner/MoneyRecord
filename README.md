# MoneyRecord

MoneyRecord is a personal finance management app that allows you to record and analyze your daily expenses by month. 

It helps you manage your personal finances by visualizing your spending through charts. In addition, travel expenses can be recorded separately. 

Beyond tracking your own spending, you can also record and analyze your friends' expenses by the end of the trip. It is convenience to split and calculate travel expenses with your friends.
